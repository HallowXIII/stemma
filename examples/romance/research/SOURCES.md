# Sources

The handbooks and reference works behind `attested.tsv` and the rules. The
references were written from memory; check edition details against a
library catalogue before citing them elsewhere.

## Consulted

Used by the research batches (full texts on archive.org) or in the
handbook pass.

- **Pope, Mildred K.** *From Latin to Modern French, with Especial
  Consideration of Anglo-Norman: Phonology and Morphology.* Manchester:
  Manchester University Press, 1934; revised ed. 1952. The handbook pass:
  final *-t* and *ei* > *oi* (dating), and the u-perfects (*awwet*,
  *krịwwịt*; the analogical *eut*, *sut*, *put*, *plut*).
- **Nyrop, Kristoffer.** *Grammaire historique de la langue française.*
  6 vols. Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1899–1930. Vols. I (phonétique) and II
  (morphologie).
- **Bourciez, Édouard.** *Précis historique de phonétique française.*
  Paris: Klincksieck, several editions from 1889. The later standard is
  Bourciez, É. and J., *Phonétique française: étude historique*,
  Klincksieck, 1967.
- **Darmesteter, Arsène.** *A Historical French Grammar.* London:
  Macmillan, 1899.
- **Menéndez Pidal, Ramón.** *Manual de gramática histórica española.*
  Madrid: Espasa-Calpe, 6th ed. 1941 (1st ed. 1904 as *Manual elemental
  de gramática histórica española*).
- **Menéndez Pidal, Ramón.** *Cantar de mio Cid: texto, gramática y
  vocabulario.* 3 vols. Madrid, 1908–1911.
- Wiktionary (English, French, Spanish), for forms and attestation dates.
- **TLFi:** *Trésor de la langue française informatisé.* ATILF (CNRS &
  Université de Lorraine). Online at atilf.fr/tlfi; readable through the
  CNRTL API (`cnrtl.fr/api/word/<form>/…`), which batch 5 used.
- **Penny, Ralph.** *A History of the Spanish Language.* 2nd ed.
  Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 2002. The handbook pass: the
  Spanish u-perfects (*ove*, *tove*, *pude*). Still to check: Old Spanish
  /b/ vs /β/, the sibilants, the 2pl *-ades* > *-áis*, the *-go* verbs.

## Still to consult

- **Lloyd, Paul M.** *From Latin to Spanish.* Vol. 1: *Historical
  Phonology and Morphology of the Spanish Language.* Philadelphia:
  American Philosophical Society, 1987 (Memoirs of the APS, 173). The
  Spanish rule order; *mn* > *mbr*; the strong perfects (*tovo*, *pudo*,
  *estido*).
- **Buridant, Claude.** *Grammaire nouvelle de l'ancien français.* Paris:
  SEDES, 2000; revised as *Grammaire du français médiéval (XIe–XIVe
  siècles)*, Strasbourg: ÉLiPhi, 2019. The Old French verb: the allegro
  forms of *faire*, *dire*, *estre* (*faimes*, *dimes*, *somes*, *estes*,
  *sui*) and the remaining strong perfects (*tint*, *fist*).
- **Einhorn, E.** *Old French: A Concise Handbook.* Cambridge: Cambridge
  University Press, 1974. Case paradigms and verb tables of c. 1150.
- **La Chaussée, François de.** *Initiation à la phonétique historique de
  l'ancien français.* Paris: Klincksieck, 1974 (2nd ed. 1989); and
  *Initiation à la morphologie historique de l'ancien français.*
  Klincksieck, 1977.
- **Zink, Gaston.** *Phonétique historique du français.* Paris: PUF, 1986.
  Dating *ai* > *ɛ* (the one dating call still unchecked), *en* > *an*,
  *l*-vocalisation.
- **FEW:** Wartburg, Walther von. *Französisches Etymologisches
  Wörterbuch.* 1922–2002. French etyma (*\*allāre*, *\*stēla*, *haut*).
- **DCECH:** Corominas, Joan, and José A. Pascual. *Diccionario crítico
  etimológico castellano e hispánico.* 6 vols. Madrid: Gredos,
  1980–1991. Spanish etyma and first attestations (*perro*, *preste*,
  *frágil*).
- **CORDE:** Real Academia Española, *Corpus diacrónico del español*
  (corpus.rae.es). Whether an Old Spanish form is attested c. 1200.
- **Modern pronunciations:** *Le Petit Robert* for French /e/ against /ɛ/
  (*aimer*, *saurai*, *mets*, *pied*); the RAE's *Diccionario de la lengua
  española*, with Navarro Tomás, *Manual de pronunciación española*, for
  Spanish.
